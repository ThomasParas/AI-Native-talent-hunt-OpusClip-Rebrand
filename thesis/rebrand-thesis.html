01 — The direction

Principle: made in the edit. Every great video is found in the cutting room, not on set. OpusClip is the editor’s bench, automated: an AI editing agent that works from your real footage. The brand should look like the place where that work happens.

Look: The Cutting Room. A light, neutral bone “editing bench” surface for reading and deciding. A dark “screening band” appears only where footage plays, so video pops. Type and CTAs are ink-black. Display type is a condensed grotesk, Archivo Condensed, echoing studio slates and film edge-codes, set over Geist body for continuity with today’s site, with Geist Mono for timecode.

The core idea is a two-ink system. In a real cutting room, editors mark film with grease pencil. We give OpusClip two pencils:

Violet #6723FF · Agent’s ink

Moments it found, scores, suggested cuts. This is OpusClip’s existing purple, so the equity is kept and given a job.

Red · Human’s ink

Approvals, edits, emphasis. Strokes and underlines only, never a button or a fill.

Every screen shows who did what. The agent proposes; the creator decides. That makes the AI legible and keeps authenticity in human hands.

Heritage at the edges, modern at the core. Cues are limited to editing artifacts anyone can read: sprocket rails as section dividers, slate headers, timecode eyebrows, grease-pencil marks, and the trim bin. The trim bin is the rack where cut film strips hang on pins, which makes it a ready-made picture of “1 long video → many clips.”

Hard ruleTheming lives only in frames, marks and transitions, and takes up ≤10% of any screen. Nav, copy, forms, pricing and the product app stay plain and modern. Cutting room, not red carpet: craft and backstage honesty, not Hollywood glamour.

Voice. Confident, plainspoken, on the creator’s side. We drop “viral” as a lead word.

02 — Why now

The product outgrew the brand. 20M+ creators and businesses in 18 months, on $50M. The product is now an AI video agent: AI Producer, Agent Opus, and an API and MCP for other agents. The brand still looks like the launch-era clipping tool.

The category is converging on prompt-to-video generation (Runway, Kapwing, Captions). OpusClip’s defensible truth is that it edits real footage of real people. The cutting room shows that truth without attacking generation. OpusClip ships generative B-roll and dubbing itself, so the brand celebrates real footage rather than condemning generated content.

The audience is broad. Creators, podcasters, marketers, agencies and media companies share one visual vocabulary: play, cut, timecode, frames. Editing artifacts speak it. For businesses, the two-ink system builds trust: they can always see what the AI did.

It scales. Product lines become reels in one system, replacing three separate logo systems:

04 — Risks we accept

  • Leaving dark mode on marketing. Light is the default, but a visitor toggle (following the OS setting) gives a dark version from the same tokens; the app stays dark.
  • Nostalgia. Modern structure and the 10% rule keep it current.
  • Descript also uses a warm light palette with red. We separate with a neutral bone (not cream), red never used as a button or fill, and a condensed sans (not a serif).
  • Cutting-room literacy. We use only universally recognized cues.
